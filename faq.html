Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about parking lot striping, scheduling, pricing, and compliance. Don't see your question? Give us a call.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the entire Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex, including Frisco, Plano, McKinney, Allen, Prosper, Dallas, Fort Worth, and surrounding cities.

How long does parking lot striping take to dry?

Standard traffic paint is typically dry to the touch within 30–60 minutes and ready for vehicle traffic in about 1–2 hours, depending on temperature and humidity.

How often should I restripe my parking lot?

Most commercial lots need restriping every 1–2 years. Heavy traffic, sun exposure, and weather all wear lines down faster. If your lines are faded, it's time.

Do you work nights or weekends to avoid business disruption?

Yes. We schedule overnight and weekend work whenever possible so your business stays open and customers aren't interrupted.

Do you handle ADA-compliant striping?

Yes. We layout and stripe ADA-compliant accessible spaces, access aisles, and pavement markings to meet current ADA standards.

Do you stripe fire lanes?

Yes. We stripe and re-mark fire lanes to local fire code, including curb painting and required lettering.

How do you price striping jobs?

Pricing depends on the size of the lot, number of stalls, type of markings, and condition of the surface. We provide a free written quote after reviewing the job.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 1–2 weeks ahead, especially in spring and fall when demand is highest. Same-week scheduling is sometimes available.

Do you do new parking lot layouts?

Yes. We design and stripe new layouts for new construction, expansions, and full lot redesigns.
